## Added functionality and why it is useful

Find & fix the mistake asks students to review a plausible but flawed analysis, identify the error, choose a valid correction, and assess its effect on the conclusion. Numerical exercises use source payroll rows and results from the supplied songs database. For example, the payroll exercise contrasts an unweighted mean of row-level hourly rates with total wages divided by total hours. Unlike checking a final answer alone, this exposes whether students recognise the population, weighting or inference error behind the result. Feedback checks the diagnosis, repair and consequence separately, allowing a retry before the worked review is revealed. A correct payroll review unlocks a fresh source sample that requires calculation without method choices. The feature is designed to support explaining and defending analysis in an oral exam; a learning benefit has not yet been measured, and it does not claim the lecturer’s AI tutor cannot discuss these errors.

## Why the starting materials make this case harder

The inputs span spreadsheets, delimited text files, SQLite databases and Word instructions across several accounting contexts. Building a useful practice site requires identifying relevant skills, preserving each dataset’s grain and field definitions, and establishing defensible answers across those formats. Some instructions also need interpretation: the IntegrateCo wording about the direction of the difference conflicts with its parenthetical explanation. Turning this material into consistent questions and diagnostic feedback therefore requires content validation and accounting judgment as well as software implementation.
